# S159 — Invite email deliverability. Investigation, no fix.

> **Brief [Josh, S159]:** *"INVITE EMAILS ARE NOT GOING THROUGH RESEND."* Report only, do not fix.
>
> **Verdict: the hypothesis is DISPROVEN for the invite email, and the instinct behind it is
> correct about something bigger sitting next to it.**
>
> The invitation Josh caught in Email Log Search **did go through Resend.** All three of its
> "structurally this is Amazon SES" signals are properties of a healthy Resend send, because Resend
> is built on SES and the address Google displayed is a bounce return-path.
>
> **But four other surfaces really do bypass Resend**, on Supabase's built-in shared mailer, with no
> SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment to `ezcontractorbinder.com`, **a project-wide cap of 2 emails per hour**,
> and no `email_logs` row. **One of them is the second half of the invite flow itself** — an invitee
> who clicks the good Resend invite is then told to wait for a Supabase confirmation email before
> they can sign in.

       └─ sendInviteEmail(supabase, id, origin)         lib/services/invite-email.ts
            ├─ invitations.select(...)                  through the CALLER's client — RLS is the gate
            ├─ companies.select(name, slug, brand_color)   via service role

`inviteUserByEmail|generateLink|admin.auth.admin` returns **two** hits in the whole application,
both unrelated: `lib/trial/lifecycle.ts:248` (`updateUserById`) and `lib/trial/deletion.ts:247`
(`deleteUser`). **There is no call to Supabase's Auth mailer anywhere in the invite flow.**

## §2 — Why the SES evidence does not mean what it looks like

Three signals were read as "this is not Resend". Each is what a **healthy Resend send** looks like.

### 2.1 The subject line could only have come from our code

Observed: `Worth Properties invited you to join them on EZ Contractor Binder`

That is `buildInviteSubject(companyName)` — `${companyName} invited you to join them on
${brand.name}` — where `companyName` is read from **our `companies` table**.

Supabase Auth's invite subject on this very project is:

```
mailer_subjects_invite: "You have been invited"
mailer_templates_invite_content: "<h2>You have been invited</h2>\n\n<p>You have been invited to create a user on {{ .SiteURL }}…"
```

GoTrue templates interpolate `{{ .SiteURL }}`, `{{ .ConfirmationURL }}`, `{{ .Token }}`, `{{ .Email }}`.
**There is no variable that could produce a tenant's company name.** Supabase Auth cannot have sent
this message.

### 2.2 `send.ezcontractorbinder.com` is Resend's own subdomain — measured, not assumed

Live DNS on the domain Josh owns and verified in Resend:

```
send.ezcontractorbinder.com   TXT   "v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all"
send.ezcontractorbinder.com   MX    10 feedback-smtp.us-east-1.amazonses.com
resend._domainkey.ezcontractorbinder.com  TXT  p=MIGfMA0GCSqGSIb3DQEBAQUAA4GNADCBiQKBgQDUMVPv/… (published)
_dmarc.ezcontractorbinder.com TXT   "v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:josh@worthprop.com"
ezcontractorbinder.com        TXT   google-site-verification=… (no SPF at the apex — correct, see below)
```

These are **exactly the records Resend asks you to publish when you verify a domain**: a custom
MAIL FROM subdomain whose SPF includes `amazonses.com`, an MX pointing at SES's feedback endpoint
for bounce handling, and a DKIM key on the `resend._domainkey` selector. **Resend runs on Amazon
SES.** That is why the `include:` says `amazonses.com` — on a subdomain of a domain Josh controls.

**Supabase's built-in mailer could not produce this envelope.** It sends from its own shared
infrastructure and has no ability to use a subdomain of a customer's domain.

### 2.3 The 60-hex local part is a bounce address, not a bulk-mail tell

```
010001a019fab4c3-2eb48afa-e35c-4388-9f79-48dc7337505c-000000@send.ezcontractorbinder.com
```

That is SES's VERP return-path format — `0100<hex>-<uuid>-000000@<mail-from-domain>` — the address
bounces and complaints are routed back to. **Every** message Resend sends has one. Google Workspace's
Email Log Search shows the **envelope sender (SMTP `MAIL FROM`)** in its Sender column, not the
`From:` header, which is why a per-message bounce id appeared where a human-readable address was
expected.

The `From:` header the recipient sees is unchanged and is what mail-tester scored:
`Worth Properties <worth-properties@ezcontractorbinder.com>`.

**The `<…@email.amazonses.com>` Message-ID is the same fact from the other end** — SES stamps the
Message-ID because SES is the MTA. It says which infrastructure carried the mail, not which product
composed it.

> **The generalisable point.** All three signals distinguish *SES from not-SES*. None of them
> distinguishes *Resend from Supabase*, because **both** would show SES — Resend by construction,
> and Supabase's built-in mailer historically too. The signal that actually separates them is the
> **subject line and the MAIL FROM domain**, and both point at Resend.

## §3 — Does any invite email go through `email-service.ts`, and is it logged?

**Yes to both, by construction.** `sendInviteEmail()` ends with an unconditional `logEmail()` —
*"Logged on success AND failure — a failed send matters most to the audit trail, and 'no email
arrived' is exactly the question this table answers."* It writes `email_type: 'invite'`,
`resend_message_id`, `status: sent|failed`, and `metadata: { invitation_id, role }`.

`invite` **is** a seeded row in `email_types` [LIVE, rebuild-test], so the FK cannot reject the log.

**On rebuild-test there are zero `invite` rows** — but no invitation has ever been created there
through the UI (its identities are seeded by script), so that is an absence of activity, not
evidence about production.

| `email_type` | sent | failed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| change_order | 12 | 3 |
| co_signature_complete | 6 | 8 |
| invoice | 2 | 0 |
| material_delivery | 20 | 186 |
| safety_incident | 30 | 268 |
| **invite** | **0** | **0** |

*(The `material_delivery` / `safety_incident` failures are harness traffic — those two harnesses run
every send path against addresses that do not exist. Not a finding here.)*

## §4 — ⚠️ THE REAL FINDING: four surfaces DO bypass Resend

`grep` for every call that makes GoTrue send mail:

| # | Surface | Call | Email the user gets | Provider |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `app/sign-up/page.tsx:25` | `supabase.auth.signUp()` | "Confirm Your Signup" | **Supabase shared SMTP** |
| 2 | `app/invite/accept/accept-invite.tsx:128` | `supabase.auth.signUp()` | "Confirm Your Signup" | **Supabase shared SMTP** |
| 3 | `app/forgot-password/page.tsx:20` | `resetPasswordForEmail()` | "Reset Your Password" | **Supabase shared SMTP** |
| 4 | `lib/services/team.ts:90` | `resetPasswordForEmail()` | "Reset Your Password" | **Supabase shared SMTP** |

None writes to `email_logs`. None is covered by any harness. None carries the company's branding,
its Reply-To, or the `From:` address mail-tester scored 10/10.

### 4.1 Production Auth configuration [Management API, read-only, 2026-08-19]

Project `jwkcknyuyvcwcdeskrmz` (**FrameFocus**, production):

```
external_email_enabled            true      ← Auth email is ON
smtp_host                         null      ← ⚠️ NO CUSTOM SMTP. The built-in shared mailer.
smtp_user / smtp_pass / smtp_port null
smtp_admin_email                  null
smtp_sender_name                  null
hook_send_email_enabled           false     ← ⚠️ the Send Email Hook, the supported route to Resend, is OFF
rate_limit_email_sent             2         ← ⚠️ 2 per hour, PROJECT-WIDE
mailer_autoconfirm                false     ← ⚠️ confirmation is REQUIRED to sign in
mailer_allow_unverified_email_sign_ins  false
mailer_otp_exp                    86400     (24h link validity)
site_url                          https://EZContractorBinder.com   (resolves, 200 — checked)
```

For comparison, rebuild-test is configured the same way (`smtp_host: null`,
`mailer_autoconfirm: false`), with `mailer_otp_exp: 3600` and `site_url: http://localhost:3000`.

### 4.2 ⚠️ The invite flow needs TWO emails, on TWO providers

This is the part that most deserves a ruling.

1. **The invitation** → `sendInviteEmail()` → **Resend**. Branded, aligned, logged, Reply-To the
   inviting company. Healthy.
2. The invitee clicks the link, lands on `/invite/accept`, sets a password, and the page calls
   `supabase.auth.signUp()`. Because `mailer_autoconfirm` is `false`, GoTrue sends a **"Confirm Your
   Signup"** email — **the Supabase shared mailer, from a domain with no relationship to
   `ezcontractorbinder.com`.** The success screen says so in as many words:

   > *"Account Created! Check your email (…) for a confirmation link. Once confirmed, you can sign
   > in and access {company}."*

3. `mailer_allow_unverified_email_sign_ins: false` means **that second email is load-bearing.**
   Without it the invitee cannot sign in at all.

**So the email most likely to be filtered, and the only one that can block an invitee, is the one
nobody has been looking at** — and it leaves no trace in `email_logs` when it fails.

### 4.3 ⚠️ `rate_limit_email_sent: 2` is per hour, for the whole project

Inviting three people in an hour means the third invitee's **confirmation** email is never sent.
Same for a burst of password resets. The invitations themselves are unaffected — Resend has its own,
far higher limits — so the failure presents as *"I got the invite, I set my password, and then
nothing."*

`accept-invite.tsx` also passes **no `emailRedirectTo`**, so that confirmation link uses `site_url`
(`https://EZContractorBinder.com`) rather than the origin the invitee was actually on. The apex
serves the app (checked: `200`), so this is not currently broken — but it is a dependency on a
config value nothing in the repo pins.

## §5 — The spam verdict: what it is not

`0/1 Delivered — Marked spam`. What has been **ruled out** by measurement:

- **Not a missing or broken SPF/DKIM/DMARC.** All three are published and aligned; mail-tester
  scores a Worth-slug send 10/10, and that is the same `From`, the same domain, the same code path.
- **Not "sent by Supabase".** §2.
- **Not the bounce-address local part.** §2.3 — every Resend message has one.
- **Not a dead link target.** `https://ezcontractorbinder.com/` returns 200.

What remains open, and is **not** determinable from this Codespace:

- `DMARC p=none` satisfies Gmail's bulk-sender minimum but asserts no policy. Moving to
  `p=quarantine` is a reputation signal, not a fix.
- **Domain age and reputation.** `ezcontractorbinder.com` is new and has almost no send history;
  a first-contact message containing a sign-up link is the highest-risk shape there is.
- **`From:` / `Reply-To:` domain mismatch.** `sendEmail()` sets Reply-To to the *company's* address
  (`josh@worthprop.com`) while `From:` is `…@ezcontractorbinder.com`. This is deliberate [S97] and
  is a mild filtering signal.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header.** `sendEmail()` sets no custom headers at all
  (`email-service.ts:290`). Transactional mail is not required to carry one; Gmail's bulk rules
  nonetheless reward it.
- The recipient was inside Josh's own Google Workspace, so tenant-level filtering rules apply and
  are invisible from outside.

**None of these is worth acting on before §6 is run.**

## §6 — The one check left, and it is decisive

Run against **production**, not rebuild-test:

```sql
select created_at, status, recipient_email, sender_email, resend_message_id, subject, metadata
from email_logs
where email_type = 'invite'
order by created_at desc
limit 20;
```

- **A row with a non-null `resend_message_id` and `status = 'sent'`** → the invite went through
  Resend, this document's conclusion is confirmed end to end, and the remaining question is purely
  Gmail reputation (§5).
- **A row with `status = 'failed'`** → read its `metadata`; the send was attempted and Resend
  refused. Different problem, fully diagnosable.
- **No row at all** → the message did not come from `sendInviteEmail()`, and §2's conclusion is
  wrong somewhere. **In that case the next place to look is not the app** — it is whether a human
  used the Supabase dashboard's *Authentication → Users → Invite* button, which sends GoTrue's own
  invite email and touches nothing in this repo.

`scripts/live-sql.mjs` refuses any project but rebuild-test and that guard was deliberately not
bypassed. The Supabase dashboard SQL editor for project `jwkcknyuyvcwcdeskrmz` is the place to run
this.

## §7 — `context100` §3 is superseded, and HEAD was established from the repo

`context100` §3 says *"There is no invite email — the form renders a copyable link, no send call
anywhere."* **That was true when written and is false at HEAD.** S135's D2 built exactly the missing
piece, and `lib/services/invite-email.ts`'s own header records the original defect in the same
words:

> *"`createInvitation()` inserted a row and `invite-form.tsx` rendered a copyable link under the
> words 'Share this link with {email}'. There was no send call anywhere, no invite function in
> `email-service.ts`, and no `invite` row in `email_types`. Josh invited two employees, neither
> received anything, and the screen gave him no reason to think anything was wrong."*

Verified at HEAD rather than inherited from either claim: the send call exists
(`invite-email.ts:sendEmail`), the `email_types` row exists [LIVE], and both routes call one shared
function.

## §8 — Proposals, for ruling. Nothing below was built.

| # | Proposal | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P1** | **Route Supabase Auth email through Resend** via the Send Email Hook (`hook_send_email_enabled` is `false`) or by setting custom SMTP to Resend's relay. | Closes all four surfaces at once, brings them under the aligned domain, and lifts the 2/hour cap. The single highest-value change here. |
| **P2** | Log Auth-originated sends to `email_logs`. | Falls out of P1 if the hook calls into `email-service.ts`. Today a failed confirmation email is invisible everywhere. |
| **P3** | Decide whether `mailer_autoconfirm` should stay `false` **for invited users**. | An invitee arrived through a link only they could have received, at an address the inviter typed. The second confirmation is arguably re-proving a fact the invitation already established — and it is the step most likely to fail. This is a security ruling, not a cleanup. |
| **P4** | Pass `emailRedirectTo` in `accept-invite.tsx`, as `sign-up/page.tsx` already does. | One-line consistency; removes a silent dependency on `site_url`. |
| **P5** | A live harness over the invite send. | `sendInviteEmail()` has none. The S135 defect it fixed was invisible for months precisely because nothing exercised it. |

**No code, schema or configuration was changed by this investigation.**
