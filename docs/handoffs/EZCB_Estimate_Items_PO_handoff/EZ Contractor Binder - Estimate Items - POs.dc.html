EZ Contractor Binder — Estimate items & purchase orders

A two-step add sheet for estimate line items, and the POs those materials become

Two connected changes. Turn 17 replaces the one-item-at-a-time add flow with a single sheet that picks a batch and then prices the batch. Turn 18 carries those material lines into purchase orders when the estimate converts to a project, categories and subcategories intact. Additions beyond today's product are badged NEW.
